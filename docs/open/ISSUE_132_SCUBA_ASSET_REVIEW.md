Use real photographs for BCD, SPG, LPI and DPV. Four locally staged candidates have verified Commons description-page author and license metadata. SCUBA and DSMB need better photographs or a custom image generated in the ChatGPT app. No SVGs, generated equipment, or runtime integration were created in this task. Do not close #132: six publishable assets and human acceptance are not complete.

The files in [`docs/open/assets/scuba-reference/`](assets/scuba-reference/) are review candidates, not accepted Learn content. ## Six-item inventory

### BCD — strong candidate

[`bcd.jpg`](assets/scuba-reference/bcd.jpg): [Buoyancy compensator.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Buoyancy_compensator.jpg), Malene Thyssen, selected license [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Visual inspection: isolated dark jacket on white, full outline and corrugated inflator assembly visible. Best of the reviewed BCD photographs for phone-size identity. Accessories in pockets and visible branding add distraction. A reviewer must confirm this older example is appropriate for the broad recreational reference and that small details are not mistaken for required equipment. Do not imply this is the only BCD form; backplate/wing configurations also exist.

### SPG — usable candidate, composition improvement desirable

[`spg.jpg`](assets/scuba-reference/spg.jpg): [Submersible pressure gauge on short HP hose P8160037.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Submersible_pressure_gauge_on_short_HP_hose_P8160037.jpg), Peter Southwood, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Visual inspection: real analog gauge with bar markings, short high-pressure hose and end fitting, lying on gray stone. Gauge is at right with considerable empty background. Device identity is visible, but dial detail will be weak in a small card. This is a short-hose example, not a generic setup prescription. Human review must assess gauge/fitting legibility in the actual app; a future crop must preserve provenance and record the change and share-alike license.

### LPI — strong close-up candidate, requires BCD context

[`lpi.jpg`](assets/scuba-reference/lpi.jpg): [Power inflator valve head for buoyancy compensator P5240188.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Power_inflator_valve_head_for_buoyancy_compensator_P5240188.jpg), Peter Southwood, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Visual inspection: large black inflator valve head, oral mouthpiece, corrugated BCD hose end and metallic low-pressure connector. Much clearer at phone size than the numbered backplate/wing image rejected below. Some connected hose extends outside the frame. Pair with the BCD reference so learners understand the assembly belongs to the BCD; do not present it as a generic hose or imply controls are identical across models. Human reviewer must confirm broad identity and that the framing is sufficient.

### DSMB — gap

Inspected [Deco buoy unfurled.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Deco_buoy_unfurled.jpg), Mark.murphy, public-domain release: 500 × 375, deflated red fabric and attached line/spool. Insufficient contrast and inflated shape context for the primary reference.

Inspected [Deployed surface marker buoy.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Deployed_surface_marker_buoy.JPG), Riley Huntley, CC BY-SA 4.0: correct surface context, but the tiny distant tube is lost at phone size. Inspected [Bottom deployable surface marker bouy P9040105.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bottom_deployable_surface_marker_bouy_P9040105.jpg), Peter Southwood, CC BY-SA 4.0: furled package, not an immediately recognizable inflated tube. None staged for delivery.

Preferred next asset: a real isolated inflated DSMB with its attachment point visible, verified as delayed-deployable rather than relying on generic SMB naming. Distinguishing DSMB from a surface-only marker needs human review.

ChatGPT app brief if photography remains unavailable: “Create a realistic reference photograph of one inflated delayed surface marker buoy, upright against a plain light neutral background, with the lower attachment point and a short portion of attached line visible. Bright orange or red elongated fabric tube. Use a supplied real DSMB photograph as the structural reference. Preserve its actual seams, lower opening/valve and attachment geometry. No diver, deployment sequence, baked-in labels, invented controls, logos or safety instruction. Frame the whole tube large enough to recognize at phone size.” A qualified reviewer must compare output with real equipment before acceptance.

### DPV — strong contextual candidate

[`dpv.jpg`](assets/scuba-reference/dpv.jpg): [Gavin scooter dab.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gavin_scooter_dab.jpg), Matthew Hoelscher, [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), originally [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/photos/26395196@N00/2715048559/).

Visual inspection: foreground diver and large cylindrical scooter with guarded propeller end and handhold, second diver behind. The DPV occupies enough foreground to read at phone size. Blue water reduces contrast; visible DAB lettering remains. Human review must confirm it teaches broad DPV identity without implying handling/configuration instruction. It is a real product example, not an endorsement.

### SCUBA — gap

Inspected [Scuba set with integral bag PA111753.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scuba_set_with_integral_bag_PA111753.JPG), Peter Southwood, CC BY-SA 3.0: green underwater image with cylinder obscured by integrated bag. Rejected for system clarity.

Inspected [Scuba 01.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scuba_01.jpg), Alexander Z., CC BY-SA 3.0: clearly photographed regulator/console assembly but no cylinder or BCD; cannot stand for the complete self-contained system.

Inspected [Scuba Diver - Ahmad Faiz Mustafa.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scuba_Diver_-_Ahmad_Faiz_Mustafa.jpg), Ahmad Faiz Mustafa, CC BY-SA 3.0: diver on boat with BCD and hoses; cylinder mostly hidden. Rejected as primary system reference. None staged for delivery.

Preferred next asset: a three-quarter rear/side photograph of an actual diver wearing a complete recreational set, with cylinder, first stage, hose path and second stage visible together.

ChatGPT app brief if photography remains unavailable: “Using supplied reference photographs of one actual recreational open-circuit scuba set, create a clean realistic three-quarter side/rear equipment reference image showing the complete system together: one cylinder mounted to a BCD/harness, regulator first stage on the cylinder valve, actual hose routing and second-stage regulator. Plain neutral background, no labels or logos. Preserve the supplied equipment geometry and connections. Show all main components with enough separation to recognize on a phone. Do not invent fittings or provide a setup sequence.” Prefer a real photo whenever the generated connections cannot be independently verified.

## Accuracy comparison and remaining work

The [SCUBAPRO BCS manual](https://scubapro.johnsonoutdoors.com/sites/default/files/2022-09/sp_stek_manual_en_0.pdf) and [SCUBA FORCE wing manual](https://www.scubaforce.eu/Portals/0/PDF/SCUBA%20FORCE%20WINGS%20MANUAL%20EN%202024.pdf) are manufacturer references for BCD/inflator form and relationship. [DAN’s DSMB explanation](https://world.dan.org/alert-diver/article/popping-the-question-what-is-a-dsmb/) is the training reference for the distinction between an SMB and DSMB. These support the review criteria; a file-description match is not mechanical acceptance. A detailed manufacturer/manual comparison for each exact candidate and independent equipment review are still pending.
